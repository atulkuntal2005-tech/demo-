About NOWMI Salon

Editorial Craftsmanship Meets Personalized Care

Welcome to NOWMI Hairdressing Salon, Bhavani’s premier unisex destination for elevated hairdressing, grooming, skincare, and celebratory beauty.

Located conveniently on Bajanai Kovil Street near TMB Bank, NOWMI brings high-touch styling into a tranquil, hygienic, and modern environment. Whether you are looking for a tailored haircut, an aromatherapeutic scalp spa, a skin-renewing detan facial, or comprehensive bridal artistry, our team takes time to consult on your hair texture and style preferences.

✓

Beauty & Detan

Sun-damage reversal, facial radiance therapies, threading, and waxing.
